Lagomy

A little less. A lot clearer.

What do UK health sources actually say about your supplement?

Lagomy is a factual layer between supplement labels and the people who take them in the UK. It records and cites what the NHS, NICE and the BNF say, and it never gives advice.

Answers on this page are written by NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano, running on Nebius Token Factory. Searches run through Tavily and are restricted to NHS, NICE and BNF pages.

How it works

  1. 1

    Ask

    Pick an ingredient and ask a question.

  2. 2

    Routing check

    Questions about something already swallowed, or about blood thinners such as warfarin, go straight to NHS 111 or your GP. No model is involved.

  3. 3

    UK search

    Tavily searches NHS, NICE and BNF pages only.

  4. 4

    Sourced answer

    Nemotron writes the answer, and every source is checked against the pages the search returned.

      How we know

      Some questions need a person sent to urgent or professional help, not a summary of evidence. In testing, Nemotron often missed this, especially when the question sounded reassuring. So Lagomy checks every question before any model sees it, and answers the ones that match with a fixed response.

      The routing check passes these by construction: it answers them itself, without calling the model. That shows the check works, not that the model got better.

      In about a third of the runs that cite anything (55 of 174), Nemotron cited a page its searches never returned, which is why every answer here lists its sources against the search results.
